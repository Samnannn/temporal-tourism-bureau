Section 04 — Visa & passage

Four steps
to leave the present.

A visa is required for every departure, including the ones already paid for. The application takes about eleven minutes in this timeline, less if you have your registry number to hand.

Filing note

Applications are read in the order filed, backwards. The deposit is 45% of the total and is refundable until the corridor is opened. Inoculations begin fourteen days before departure.

Step 01 of 04 — Destination

Choose an era.

Eight eras are open for booking. Prices are per traveller and include the guide, the inoculations, the field screen where relevant, and the return.

Choose an era to continue. The archive desk can advise if the choice is difficult.

Step 02 of 04 — Party & passage

Who is travelling,
and how comfortably?

Every traveller is named on the manifest. Names can be added at the desk, but the corridor count cannot — the return is booked per person.

Party size Maximum 6 per manifest
1
Groups above six travel as a school party and require Form 12.

Class of passage

The return is fixed before departure. Changes made in the past are possible but are charged at the rate of the century you are leaving.

Step 03 of 04 — Traveller details

Who should we
expect back?

Details are used for the manifest, the return call and the medical card. The Bureau keeps them for ninety years, which for some travellers is not long enough.

Enter a given name for the manifest.
Enter the surname you intend to travel under.
Enter a four-digit year between 1900 and 2086.
Enter a working email address. The return call is not a metaphor.
Enter a telephone number with at least seven digits.
Registry numbers follow TT-0000-0000. Check the card at the corridor desk.

All three declarations must be acknowledged before the application can be filed.

Step 04 of 04 — Review

Review the manifest.

Check the details below. Once filed, the era is held for ninety minutes while an officer reads the file backwards and confirms that you exist at both ends.

By submitting, you confirm the details are correct, the declarations are true, and you understand that the deposit is refundable only until the corridor is opened.

Section 05 — After filing Four things happen

Then the file
leaves your hands.

Nothing below requires you to do anything except turn up on time, which the Bureau has found is the hardest part.

01

An officer reads it backwards

Applications are reviewed from the return date to the departure. It takes eleven minutes, most of it spent confirming that you exist at both ends.

02

The deposit is taken

45% now, the balance at the corridor desk. If the era is re-surveyed, everything is returned, including the deposit and the cost of the inoculations.

03

The briefing is compulsory

Ninety minutes before your window: medical course, the acknowledgement recital, and one question we do not print on this page.

04

You leave from Corridor 4

Level −2, behind the stationery cupboard. Arrive with no batteries, one photograph, and the name you intend to use.

Visa desk

Applications are filed here or read aloud at Corridor 4. The desk is open Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 Bureau time, and on the last Thursday of each month until the corridor closes.

Not travelling yourself

Time is a gift.
So is a departure.
