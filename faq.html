Section 08 — The paradox desk

Questions nobody
has asked yet.

The paradox desk answers in the order questions arrive, which is not always the order they were asked. These are the twelve we are asked most often, transcribed without the sigh.

Desk note

Answers apply to civilian travel under Licence TT-0041. They do not apply to diplomats, historians travelling on a research waiver, or the one traveller who returned twice.

Response time four minutes

Before you go

01Can I kill someone who deserves it?

No. The Bureau does not sell assassination packages, and the six requests on file were all declined. Four were withdrawn by the applicant after the briefing; the other two are still in the room, deciding.

02What happens if I meet myself?

Follow Form 88, printed in full on the advisories page: do not shake hands, say nothing about the future, and leave within four minutes. If it happens twice, stop travelling.

Forty-one self-encounters are on record. None were fatal. Nine ended in a change of career, usually toward the civil service.

03Will my phone work?

No. It will be held at the corridor desk and returned with a stamped chit. In most eras there is nothing to connect to. In 2041 there is, briefly, which is worse.

04Can I bring back a souvenir?

Yes, if it is certified inert and date-safe. The Bureau shop sells replicas, clearly stamped as replicas. Anything with a date, a portrait, or a chance of being recognised stays where it was made.

05Which era is best for a first trip?

The Last Connected Afternoon, 2041. It is a single afternoon, nothing in it can hurt you, and the guide is largely there to stop you from talking.

The Mughal Court is the better story and the worse first choice.

While you are there

06What happens if I change one small thing?

It depends on the size of the thing and the century's appetite for it. Most changes are absorbed without comment, the way a river absorbs a hand.

One change is a Level 4 incident. That file is sealed, and the traveller now works for us.

07Am I allowed to eat the food?

Yes, and you should accept one item even if you are not hungry. Declining food is remembered longer than you are.

The Bureau inoculates you against eleven extinct diseases. It cannot inoculate you against being a poor guest.

08Why can't I visit next Tuesday?

Because next Tuesday is still being built. The Bureau only opens eras that have finished arriving. This is also why we cannot sell you a trip to last week, though we are asked daily.

09Is the past already decided?

The official position is that the past is settled and the traveller is temporary. Unofficially, the Board keeps a drawer of incidents that suggests the position is a form of politeness.

After you return

10What if I don't come back?

The return guarantee covers ninety minutes of drift. If you are still not back after that, your fare is refunded, your family is notified gently, and a file is opened.

One traveller, in 1988, came back twice. We still send birthday cards.

11Can I attend my own funeral?

No. This is the most common request the desk refuses and the least argued. The Bureau will not issue a visa for an event at which your presence is the incident.

12Do you offer student discounts?

Yes — 15% on eras rated Level 1 or 2, with a valid student card from any century. The card must be laminated in your home era. We cannot accept a photograph of a card, for reasons the applicant usually works out at the desk.
