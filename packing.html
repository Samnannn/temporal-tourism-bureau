Section 05 — Kit & conduct

Everything you need,
which is almost nothing.

The Bureau issues the coat, the boots and the coin. You bring a photograph, a pencil and a name. Everything else is either provided or prohibited, sometimes both.

Allowance

One bag, seven kilograms, no batteries. The bag is returned to you at the end of the visit, usually with less in it. Anything the Bureau issues is fitted at the corridor desk and returned at the gate.
